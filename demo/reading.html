WORD MEMORY / READING PRACTICE

Where words
begin to stay.

We remember a word more easily when it belongs to a story. Memory is not a cabinet of isolated facts; it is a network of connections.

To discover the origin of a word is to follow a small thread through history. Curiosity turns an unfamiliar expression into something worth keeping.

安装扩展后，双击或拖动选中 remember、memory、origin 或 curiosity，再点击「查词」。词卡会保留你读到的原句。此页面也用于检查网页样式是否被扩展干扰。
