# Data sources and attribution

The extension fetches dictionary data on demand. It does not bundle a complete dictionary. Each displayed entry retains clickable source and license information.

- **Free Dictionary API** — https://dictionaryapi.dev/ ; API: https://api.dictionaryapi.dev/api/v2/entries/en/WORD . Entry content commonly originates from English Wiktionary; preserve each response's license and source URLs. The remember test response reports CC BY-SA 3.0. Pronunciation recordings have their own Wikimedia source/license fields; follow the original entry and recording page before redistributing audio. Pronunciation uses installed system voices; the extension does not stream or bundle these recordings.
- **English Wiktionary** — https://en.wiktionary.org/ . Text is made available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 and the GNU Free Documentation License, subject to the original page's notices: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights . Rendered etymology and term extracts are adaptations produced by removing wiki markup and limiting length. Original page links are retained. Historical reconstruction markers and multiple etymology sections remain distinguishable.
- **Kaikki / Wiktextract** — https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/ . Structured extracts of English Wiktionary, generated by Wiktextract. The extension fetches per-word JSONL entries as a parallel fallback and preserves both the original Wiktionary entry and the Kaikki page links. Source entry licenses apply; see https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/index.html . Structured extraction is a transformed representation and may lag the original entry.
- **MyMemory / Translated** — https://mymemory.translated.net/ ; public API documentation: https://mymemory.translated.net/doc/spec.php . Optional machine / translation-memory results are explicitly identified as automatic translation and may be inaccurate or rate limited.

The tests/fixtures/remember-* files (including remember-kaikki.jsonl) are captured API responses from the above dictionary and Wiktionary remember entry, collected 2026-11-17; original source pages and applicable licenses are as above. Test fixtures are not included in the installable archive.

- **Noto Sans IPA font subset** — Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic), SIL Open Font License 1.1. The locally bundled subset covers Latin, IPA and combining marks; it is obtained from Google Fonts and used only to render phonetic text. Full license: extension/fonts/OFL.txt. No remote font requests are made during use.

The general dictionary fixture was captured from Free Dictionary API on 2026-11-18; its response retains source and license information.
